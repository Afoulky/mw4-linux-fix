License and source
===================

runtime/d3d12.dll and runtime/d3d12core.dll are a build of vkd3d-proton
(LGPL-2.1-or-later) with small changes; dxil-spirv (MIT) is part of it. You
can replace them with your own build: put your d3d12.dll and d3d12core.dll
in runtime/ (mw4-launch copies them into sp26/ on the next launch).
Everything else in this folder (mw4-launch, sp26/mw4-launch, bin/codpatch,
runtime/ensure_steamid.py) was written for this project and contains no game
files. "Call of Duty" is a trademark of
Activision Publishing, Inc.; this project is not affiliated with it.

Changes (included in this folder, in source/)
  source/vkd3d-proton-small-primitive-cull.patch
                vkd3d-proton: include/vkd3d_shader.h,
                libs/vkd3d-shader/dxil.c, libs/vkd3d/device_workarounds.c.
                A new shader quirk that turns off the small-primitive cull in
                the two camera triangle-culling shaders of this game, and the
                shader quirk "prefix_sum_lookback" = FORCE_MIN_WAVE32 together
                with a change in libs/vkd3d-shader/dxil.c that tells dxil-spirv
                the minimum subgroup size is 32 for such shaders (about 120
                added lines).
  source/dxil-spirv-fixes.patch
                dxil-spirv: cfg_structurizer.cpp,
                opcodes/dxil/dxil_resources.cpp. Three small fixes (12 added
                lines in total).
  The patches are the exact difference between the upstream commits above
  and the tree the DLLs were built from.

Build
  Apply the two patches to the two upstream commits (dxil-spirv is the
  submodule of vkd3d-proton), then build with the vkd3d-proton script
  package-release.sh (it needs the mingw-w64 cross compiler, meson and
  ninja). The DLLs in this folder were then stripped with
      x86_64-w64-mingw32-strip --strip-unneeded
